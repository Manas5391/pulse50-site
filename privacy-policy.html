Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Pulse50 News ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our website (https://dashing-manatee-b344df.netlify.app) and Android application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration or collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, phone number, or GPS location) to browse news stories. Selected state preferences, cached feeds, and reader comments are stored locally on your device.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to Pulse50 News and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

3. External News Publishers & Embedded Media

News cards link to verified third-party publishers and may include embedded video players (such as YouTube). When you open external articles or play videos, those third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Pulse50 News is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
