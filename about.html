About Pulse50 News & Editorial Policy

Pulse50 News is an independent digital news platform delivering concise, original 50-word news summaries across General News, India National, World Affairs, all 28 Indian States, Politics, Sports, Entertainment, and Podcasts.

How Our 50-Word Editorial Desk Works

Every news card published on Pulse50 is processed through our editorial desk to present verified public developments in clear, complete sentences capped strictly at 50 words. Each brief includes source attribution and a direct link to read the full report on the primary publisher's website.
